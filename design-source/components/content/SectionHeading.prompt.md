One-line: every section starts with one of these — sentence case, and the second line handwritten if the heading needs a lift.

```jsx
<SectionHeading title="Co robimy" lead="kawa na każdą sytuację, serio każdą" />
<SectionHeading eyebrow="trochę o nas" title="B. Coffee" hand="— be happy" size="lg" />
<SectionHeading title="Obserwuj nas," hand="bądź na bieżąco" size="md" />
```

Do not uppercase the title, and do not add a rule or divider under it — the air below is the separator.
