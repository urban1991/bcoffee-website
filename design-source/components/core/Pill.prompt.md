One-line: the eyebrow capsule — one short uppercase line above a headline, or a small link label.

```jsx
<Pill>Kawiarnia na kółkach</Pill>
<Pill tone="onPhoto">Kawiarnia na kółkach</Pill>
<Pill tone="outline" uppercase={false} href="https://www.instagram.com/bcoffeebehappy/">@bcoffeebehappy</Pill>
```

Never stack two pills, and never use one as a button — clickable things are `Button`.
