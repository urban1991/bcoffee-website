One-line: the turquoise scrolling band that separates the hero from the rest of the page — one per page, never two.

```jsx
<Ticker items={['KAWA NA EVENT', 'BARISTA NA TARGI', 'KAWA NA WESELE', 'BAR Z LEMONIADĄ', 'BE HAPPY']} />
```

Items are uppercase because this is signage, not copy. Keep `duration` at 30s.
